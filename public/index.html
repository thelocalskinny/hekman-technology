Our first application

Book Please

Your Ultimate Reading Second Brain

Keep your books, reading, listening, notes, and history connected in one personal library.

Visit Book Please ↗
01

Read and listen in one place

Use built-in EPUB and audiobook controls. Listening and reading progress stays in step with manual updates, book profiles, and reading history.

02

Add a whole shelf at once

Take a photo of a stack or shelf, or paste a book-list link. Review the matches and choose where each book goes in your library.

03

Remember the story so far

With an EPUB attached, get a searchable AI guide to characters and places that grows as you read, for quick reminders without spoilers.

04

Keep notes that mean something

Save a note on a specific book and tag it—for example, “My sister recommended this” tagged “friend referral.” Fun fact: my sister suggested this feature.

05

Share a profile on your terms

Show detailed stats, custom lists, reading history, and automatic Books of the Month. Turn any profile section off whenever you like.

06

Your library stays yours

Your book library lives in your own Google Drive. Connect Claude or ChatGPT to work with your reading data on your terms.

07

Choose what to read next

Set a priority for books on your Want to Read shelf and bring your most tempting picks to the top.

08

Find books your way

Combine detailed filters for status, tags, moods, formats, ratings, dates, reviews, and more.

09

Make the app yours

Personal settings give you control over the look and behavior of Book Please and its many features.

10

Keep big results manageable

Expand the search results you want to explore and tuck the rest away.

11

All the tracker essentials

Set goals, take on challenges, explore stats and reading history, scan a book’s ISBN barcode, and follow friends.
